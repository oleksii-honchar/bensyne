# Information about the company
1. How long has the company existed?
2. Does it have a mission and values?
3. Who will be your manager? Are you comfortable with them? And who is the boss's boss? How long has your future manager been here?
4. How does the company make money? What are its profitable products or services?
# Working conditions
1. What is the work schedule?
2. Is remote work possible? Or only office.
3. What is the salary? Is the amount written in Gross or Net? White salary or not quite? How exactly is it written?
4. How often is the salary raised in the company?
5. Are there bonuses? What do they depend on? How exactly will they be written in the offer?
6. Is there a social package? What does it include?
# Job duties
1. What is the name of your position? What is your grade? If the company has a grading system.
2. Are the duties written for formality or are they what you actually need to do?
3. If there are goals for the probation period, are all these goals really achievable in 3 months of work?

# GPT Recommendation
1. **What are the biggest challenges that the company or team is facing currently?**
2. **What is the company culture like?**
3. **What is the management style of the team/department?**
4. What are the primary responsibilities and duties of this position?
